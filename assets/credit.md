I created all of the assets for this game. If you are here, you are probably interested in how I created them.
- sfx/ - https://www.bfxr.net/
- voice/ - I generated text with a markov chain generator https://github.com/void-scape/markov and recorded myself reciting the text in voice/script.md, then applied some granular synthesis https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bmsz5JNi2Mo
- images/ - The fractals in here I rendered with https://github.com/void-scape/fanim
- music/ - A mixture of presets from various Reaktor 6 synths
